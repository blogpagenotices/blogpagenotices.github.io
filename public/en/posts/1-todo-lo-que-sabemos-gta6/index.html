Everything we know about GTA 6 so far (and why it is not just rumors anymore)

Release date, price, editions, protagonists and map: we cover everything officially confirmed about GTA 6 ahead of launch.

For years, everything we had on GTA 6 was rumors, leaked footage, and forum theories. That has changed. Rockstar Games has been confirming, one by one, the details millions of players were waiting for. Here’s the full, up-to-date rundown, no filler.

1. The date is no longer a rumor: November 19, 2026#

After an initial delay in 2025, Rockstar officially confirmed that GTA 6 launches on November 19, 2026. This time the date is backed by the official opening of pre-orders, so the odds of another postponement are low.

2. PS5 and Xbox only… for now#

The initial launch is exclusive to PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The PC version still has no confirmed date — if the pattern repeats like it did with GTA V, it could arrive 6 to 12 months after the console launch.

3. The price that split the fanbase#

Rockstar confirmed two editions:

  • Standard Edition: $79.99 USD / €79.99 EUR
  • Ultimate Edition: $99.99 USD / €99.99 EUR (includes exclusive vehicles, weapons, and outfits)

It’s the first time a mainline GTA breaks the traditional $60-70 price barrier, reflecting rising development costs in recent years.

4. The “physical” version doesn’t come with a disc#

Buyers of the boxed edition will only get a download code, not a physical disc. Fully digital formats are gaining ground even in retail editions.

5. Pre-ordering comes with gifts#

Anyone who pre-orders before launch gets the Vintage Vice City Pack (cosmetics inspired by the original GTA Vice City) and, for digital pre-orders, a free month of GTA+.

6. Vice City is back, but it’s not alone#

The game takes place in Leonida, a modern fictional version of Florida, with Vice City as its main city. The map is expected to be considerably bigger than GTA V’s, with rural areas, swamps, and several islands.

7. Two protagonists, a historic first#

For the first time in a mainline GTA, the story follows a protagonist duo: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Lucia is also the first playable female lead in the entire franchise’s history.

8. Total silence on the online mode#

When announcing pricing and editions, Rockstar only talked about the single-player experience. There has been no official mention of a new online mode — something that hasn’t gone unnoticed by a community still playing GTA Online, thirteen years after GTA V.

What happens now?#

With the date, price, and editions already confirmed, what’s left is waiting for more gameplay details in the coming weeks. We’ll keep updating this guide as Rockstar shares more information — check back soon.
