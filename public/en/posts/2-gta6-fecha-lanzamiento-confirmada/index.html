GTA 6 finally has an official release date: November 19, 2026

Rockstar Games officially confirmed that Grand Theft Auto VI will launch on November 19, 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

The wait is finally over#

After years of rumors, leaks, and multiple delays, Rockstar Games officially confirmed that Grand Theft Auto VI will launch on November 19, 2026. The confirmation comes from Take-Two Interactive, Rockstar’s parent company, putting an end to any doubts about another delay.

Launch platforms#

The initial release will be available exclusively on:

  • PlayStation 5
  • Xbox Series X|S

Rockstar has not announced a release date for the PC version. Most industry analysts expect that version to arrive later, possibly during 2027, following the same pattern GTA V had back in the day.

Vice City and Leonida: the setting#

The game takes place in a modern version of Florida, within the fictional state of Leonida, with Vice City as its narrative and visual centerpiece. Rockstar already released a first cover art showing a collage of action scenes: motorcycle chases, an alligator emerging from the water, a scissor-door sports car, and a speedboat at full throttle.

The protagonists: Jason and Lucia#

For the first time in the main series, GTA 6 features a protagonist duo: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, with the latter being the first playable female lead in the franchise’s history.

What comes next?#

With the release date now confirmed, Rockstar is expected to share more details in the coming months about pricing, special editions, and how the online mode will work. We’ll keep following this coverage closely on the blog.
