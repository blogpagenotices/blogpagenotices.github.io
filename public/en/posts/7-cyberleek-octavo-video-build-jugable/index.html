Eight GTA 6 leaked videos and counting: CyberLeek claims to have a full playable build

New leaks show Jason in a strip club and driving at high speed. CyberLeek claims to have a playable build distributed across multiple locations worldwide, though part of this latest claim remains unconfirmed.

The eighth (and ninth) video are already circulating#

The wave of leaks isn’t slowing down. The latest clips show Jason Duval robbing a gas station, driving at high speed for nearly two minutes, flying a small plane over the city and, in the most talked-about one, walking into a strip club in Vice City. In one video, the leaker themselves spells out the word “Leek” inside the game using bullet holes on a wall — a way of proving they have direct access to a playable build, not just loose recordings.

The threat that deserves some skepticism#

A message attributed to CyberLeek is circulating claiming they not only have a complete playable build, but already distributed copies across various locations worldwide so Rockstar can’t easily contain it. This is where we need to slow down a bit: several specialized outlets point out that this particular claim may be coming from a fake account impersonating CyberLeek, capitalizing on the confusion caused by the leak wave. The leaker’s own circle had already warned about impostor profiles spreading AI-generated content. Until there’s confirmation from a recognized CyberLeek channel, this specific threat should be treated as unverified.

Take-Two widens the legal net#

What is confirmed: Take-Two no longer just requested information from Microsoft and Discord — it also legally demanded that X (Twitter) and Google hand over data on accounts linked to distributing the material, with a deadline of September 4. The company is racing against the clock to identify the person responsible before that date.

CyberLeek’s site goes down#

In a turn that could mark the end of this particular wave, on the morning of August 22 CyberLeek’s website and Telegram channel stopped being available. Anyone trying to access the Telegram channel now finds a copyright infringement message. Without its main distribution infrastructure, the pace of leaks may slow down considerably — though, as we’ve already seen, this group has managed to resurface before.

The context worth keeping in mind#

All of this is happening just days before Rockstar officially presents “Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look” on August 27 on Netflix. The question the whole community is asking right now is simple: does CyberLeek have anything genuinely “new” left to show after the official event, or has the most important material already leaked before Rockstar could reveal it in its own voice?

For the full recap of everything that’s happened with this case, check out our previous article on the legal escalation.
