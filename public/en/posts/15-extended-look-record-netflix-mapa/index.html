GTA 6 just broke a Netflix record even Rockstar didn't see coming, and the map's size explains why

The Extended Look became the number 1 most-watched title on Netflix, beating out movie premieres. It was also confirmed that GTA 6's map will be about three times bigger than Red Dead Redemption 2's.

A phenomenon you can actually measure#

It’s no longer just a vague sense that “everyone is talking about GTA 6” — there’s now hard data confirming it. According to Sensor Tower figures, “Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look” ranked as the #1 most-watched title on Netflix, outperforming even movie premieres on the platform. For a video that isn’t even a traditional series or film, but a video game preview, that’s a number few people — not even inside Rockstar itself — expected to see.

Traffic spiked within hours#

It wasn’t just viewership inside Netflix. According to reports, both web traffic and Netflix’s mobile app usage spiked in the hours following the premiere. And that’s despite the stream being, in the words of several outlets, “an unusual move”: Netflix held the video exclusively for a full six hours before it landed on YouTube and Rockstar’s official site, around 3:00 AM (Spanish peninsular time) on August 28.

It’s already free on YouTube#

If you don’t have Netflix, you no longer need it — the 27-minute video has been available for free on Rockstar Games’ official YouTube channel since this very morning. It’s worth clarifying the actual focus of the material: it’s not a mechanics tutorial or a weapons breakdown, but a piece built to sell feeling and atmosphere — neon lights, endless roads, and a protagonist duo Rockstar clearly wants you to fall for before letting you loose in Vice City.

The detail that changes the scale of everything: the map size#

Here’s the reveal that will likely have the most lasting impact from the entire event: it was confirmed that GTA 6’s map will be roughly three times bigger than Red Dead Redemption 2’s — a game that, for context, is already considered one of the largest open worlds ever built in the industry. If that figure holds, GTA 6 wouldn’t just be the biggest map in the series’ history, but potentially one of the biggest in the entire open-world genre.

A visual detail that didn’t go unnoticed#

Early reactions to the graphics were also overwhelmingly positive, with one specific detail catching critics’ attention: the variety of body types among secondary characters in the world — something very few games, even at this budget level, manage to depict with this much consistency. Combined with the photorealistic faces and natural animations, the general takeaway is that the graphical leap over GTA V is significant.

What this means heading into November#

With this level of media buzz already locked in months before launch, and with Rockstar having regained control of the narrative after weeks of leaks (as we covered in our analysis of Rockstar’s official response), everything points to November not just being the launch of another video game — it’s very likely shaping up to be one of the biggest entertainment events of the year, period.
