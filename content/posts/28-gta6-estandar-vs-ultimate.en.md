## Two editions to choose from

**GTA 6 has just two editions**: **Standard** and **Ultimate**. The game launches on November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and which one you buy affects what you can customize in the story. This is what Rockstar says on its official editions page, updated on November 4.

## What each edition includes

| | **Standard** | **Ultimate** |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | $79.99 | $99.99 |
| **Full game** | Yes | Yes |
| **Ultimate Edition Upgrade** | No | Yes, with 16 extras |
| **Vintage Vice City Pack** | Yes, if pre-ordered or bought before November 20 | Yes, if pre-ordered or bought before November 20 |
| **One month of GTA+** | Yes, on digital purchases before November 20 | Yes, on digital purchases before November 20 |

Prices are those reported by outlets like PC Gamer and TechRadar; local prices in your country may differ, so check your store.

## What the Ultimate upgrade adds

According to Rockstar, the **Ultimate Edition Upgrade** adds 16 extras spread through Jason and Lucia's story, with new items unlocked after completing each chapter. They include:

- **Vehicles:** the '95 Grotti Cheetah, the Ganado Retro Build and vehicles from Jason's hideout.
- **Weapons:** Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers and custom weapon variants.
- **Style and shops:** Vice City Styles, Goodtime Gear, Rideout Customs and Sara's Unisex Salon.
- **Collection:** the Classic Car Collection.

Outlets that break down the full list mention other vehicles and shops, such as the Shitzu Squalo and the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy, but in this article we only treat as confirmed what appears on Rockstar's page.

## What the Vintage Vice City Pack includes

This is the pre-order bonus. According to Rockstar, it brings the **'55 Vapid Stanier**, outfits and hairstyles for the protagonists and an exclusive weapon pattern. Anyone who pre-orders or buys either edition **before November 20** gets it.

## What about physical editions?

- The box contains a **download code, no disc**.
- They go on sale from **November 12**, for shipping or in-store pickup.
- They include the game code and the Vintage Vice City Pack code. You can use the code as soon as you have it, for the preload that is already open.
- **There is no physical Ultimate version.** Buyers of physical Standard must buy the upgrade on the digital store.
- Rockstar does not mention the GTA+ month for physical versions.

## Can I upgrade to Ultimate later?

Yes. According to Rockstar, the **Ultimate Edition Upgrade** can be bought as a digital add-on on the PlayStation or Microsoft stores, at any time after redeeming the Standard code. We could not find the price of that upgrade, so we cannot say how much it would cost separately.

## How to decide

- **Pick Standard** if you care about the story and the open world, and do not mind skipping the customization extras. You can always upgrade later.
- **Pick Ultimate** if you want everything from day one: exclusive vehicles, weapons, styles and shops.
- **If you buy physical**, remember only Standard exists, and the upgrade is separate, digital.

This is not a purchase recommendation: it depends on your budget and how much you care about customization.
