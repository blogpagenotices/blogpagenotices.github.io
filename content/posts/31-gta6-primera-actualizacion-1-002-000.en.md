## Rockstar released an update six days before launch

On November 13, 2026, with no prior announcement, Rockstar Games released the **first GTA 6 update**, version **01.002.000**, for anyone who already preloaded. It is **about 1 GB** (1.07 GB on PS5, according to Beebom) and installs on top of the game's 212 GB. Rockstar **published no patch notes**, so there is no official explanation of what changes. Here is what is known and what is not.

## What we do know

- **Version and size:** 01.002.000, roughly 1 GB.
- **No official notes:** Rockstar did not explain what it contains.
- **Phased rollout:** according to ComicBook and Tech Times, about half of players got it first and the rest about 11 hours later. That is why it may not have shown up for you yet.
- **Platforms:** Beebom reports it on PS5. ComicBook and Tech Times say it also reached Xbox Series X, but Beebom notes there is no confirmation for Xbox Series X|S.
- **Mandatory:** ComicBook and Tech Times say it is needed to play on launch day. Rockstar has not confirmed it itself, so treat it as the most likely scenario, not a fact.

## What changes in the files (and why it is only a hint)

Users who tracked the game's data, as ComicBook reports citing iGrandTheftAuto, noticed this:

| Package | Size change |
|---|---|
| story000-[GameContent] | about 15.75 MiB |
| Levels[Americas]-0 | about 35.75 MiB |
| Audio, movies and textures | unchanged |

That points to a change touching the **single-player campaign** and the **game world**, but **we do not know what it fixes**. One possible reading is a targeted fix to missions or map areas, but that is an inference, not a confirmation. There is also no confirmed link with other news this week.

## What to do now

1. **Check for the update manually.** On PS5, highlight the game on the home screen, press the options button and choose check for update. On Xbox, go to My games & apps and check pending updates. Menu names can vary with your system version.
2. **Download it early.** It is small, but better to have it ready before November 19, especially on a slow connection.
3. **Leave your console ready.** Turn on automatic updates and keep the console connected so it receives future patches if there are any.
4. **If it does not show up, do not worry.** The rollout is phased and it may reach you a few hours later.
5. **Do not assume it is the last one.** Rockstar has not said whether there will be more patches before launch.
