## A side effect nobody saw coming

When we covered the [572% surge in GTA 6 pre-orders](/en/posts/16-reservas-disparadas-extended-look/) following the Extended Look, we briefly mentioned a detail that deserves its own breakdown: **PlayStation 5 Pro** units started selling out at several retailers, with resale prices climbing as high as **$1,300** — more than triple the console's official price.

## Why this is happening right now

GTA 6 doesn't need a PS5 Pro to run — the standard PS5 handles the game just fine. But a segment of the most enthusiastic players is treating the launch as the perfect excuse to upgrade their hardware, chasing the best possible experience (higher resolution, better performance) from day one. When that impulse combines with demand that was already high for the console itself, the result is what we're seeing: empty shelves.

## Is it worth overpaying right now?

This calls for some caution. Launch is still more than two months away (November 19), and it's reasonable to expect that:

- Retailers will restock in the coming weeks, as usually happens after short-term demand spikes
- Rockstar hasn't confirmed official technical requirements yet, so there's no guarantee the PS5 Pro will offer a dramatic difference over the standard PS5 for this specific game
- Paying a resale markup now, when there's still plenty of time, is rarely the most sensible decision from a purely financial standpoint

## The bigger picture

Beyond whether it makes sense to buy right now, this shortage is one more confirmation of something we've been documenting throughout our coverage: interest in GTA 6 has stopped being just "social media noise" and is already translating into real purchasing decisions, with measurable consequences in the hardware market.
