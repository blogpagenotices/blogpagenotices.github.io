## How much space you need for GTA 6

Rockstar confirmed that **GTA 6 will take up to 212 GB** and that preload starts on **November 10**. Before downloading, the first step is knowing how much free space you have. Here is what to keep in mind:

- **Official figure:** up to **212 GB**, for PS5 and Xbox. Rockstar did not give separate per-console figures.
- **PS5 (unofficial):** an account that tracks game sizes reported 210 GB. It is a report, not an announcement.
- **Xbox (unofficial):** the Xbox Store listing reportedly shows a maximum install size of **225.3 GB**, about 13 GB above Rockstar's figure. Rockstar has not explained the gap.
- **Launch patch:** not confirmed. If there is one, it would add to that 212 GB.

Our recommendation: **plan for about 225 GB free on Xbox and at least 215 GB on PS5**, with some extra room for updates.

## How much usable space your console has

The storage a console advertises is not what you can actually use, because the system takes a share. These are approximate usable figures:

| Console | Advertised storage | Approximate usable space |
|---|---|---|
| PS5 (original model) | 825 GB | about 667 GB |
| PS5 Slim | 1 TB | about 842 GB |
| PS5 Pro | 2 TB | about 1.86 TB |
| Xbox Series X | 1 TB | about 802 GB |
| Xbox Series S | 512 GB | about 364 GB |

If you have a **512 GB Xbox Series S**, note that GTA 6 would take more than half of your usable space (212 GB out of about 364 GB). You may need to uninstall a lot or expand your storage.

## How to check your free space

- **PS5:** open Settings and go to Storage. You will see free space and what each game takes.
- **Xbox:** open Settings, then System, then Storage devices (on some versions it shows simply as Storage).

Menu names can vary slightly with your language and system version.

## What to delete first

1. **Games you have not opened in months.** A practical rule: if it has been six months, uninstall it. You can reinstall it later from your library.
2. **Screenshots and videos.** Clip folders weigh more than they look. Upload them to the cloud or a USB drive before deleting.
3. **Big games you have already finished.** Shooters and open-world games often take tens of GB each.
4. **Apps you do not use.**

Before uninstalling a game, make sure your saves are backed up to the cloud. On Xbox they sync with your account. On PS5, cloud backup requires a PlayStation Plus subscription; if you do not have one, copy your saves to a USB drive.

## What to do if your console falls short

- **PS5:** you can expand storage with an M.2 NVMe PCIe Gen 4 SSD. Guides recommend read speeds of 5,500 MB/s or more. A USB external drive **cannot run** PS5 games; it can only store them.
- **Xbox Series X|S:** to play current-generation games you need a **storage expansion card**. A USB drive can only store them, not run them.
- **Without buying anything:** uninstall your heaviest games, even temporarily, and reinstall them after launch.

There is no need to rush out and buy a drive today: you can preload as soon as you have the space, and the game unlocks on the 19th.

## When preload starts

- **Xbox Series X|S:** 12:00 a.m. GMT on November 10. In US Eastern time that is **November 9 at 7:00 p.m.**
- **PS5:** 12:00 a.m. on November 10, in your local time.

Details by country are in our guide on [when preload starts, by platform](/en/posts/24-gta6-precarga-10-noviembre-212-gb/), and the unlock time in [what time GTA 6 comes out in your country](/en/posts/25-gta6-a-que-hora-sale-por-pais/).

## Quick checklist before preload

1. Check your free space on the console.
2. Free up until you have about 225 GB, or more if you can.
3. Back up your saved games.
4. Check that your digital pre-order is in your library.
5. Connect the console by cable if possible and turn off rest mode during the download.
