## Rockstar moved preload up

If you were waiting for November 12 to start downloading **GTA 6**, plans have changed: Rockstar Games moved preload up to **November 10, 2026**, nine days before the **November 19** launch. It also confirmed how much space you will need: **up to 212 GB**. Here is everything that matters, in order.

## When does preload start, by platform

According to Rockstar's announcement, as reported by outlets like Notebookcheck, gHacks and Techloy, the start times are:

| Platform | Preload start |
|---|---|
| **PS5** | November 10, 12:00 a.m. (midnight) **local time** |
| **Xbox Series X\|S** | November 10, 12:00 a.m. **GMT** |
| **Physical edition (download code)** | November 12 |

Because Xbox uses GMT rather than local time, the start depends on where you live. Our conversions (check on your console before bed):

| Region | Xbox Series X\|S |
|---|---|
| US Eastern | November 9, 7:00 p.m. |
| US Pacific | November 9, 4:00 p.m. |
| UK | November 10, 12:00 a.m. |
| Central Europe | November 10, 1:00 a.m. |

On PS5, preload arrives at midnight in **your** time zone: November 10 at 12:00 a.m., wherever you are.

## How big is GTA 6: up to 212 GB

Rockstar says the game will need **up to 212 GB** of storage and notes the figure may vary by device. There is no per-console breakdown. Before the announcement, a leak pointed to about 210 GB on PS5, which turned out to be close to the official figure.

For perspective, that is more than double GTA V Enhanced on PS5 (around 87 GB). Leave some extra room for launch patches and updates, too.

## How long will the download take

Theoretical times for 212 GB on a stable connection, ignoring other downloads and interruptions:

- **25 Mbps:** about 19 hours.
- **50 Mbps:** about 9.5 hours.
- **100 Mbps:** about 4 hours 45 minutes.
- **300 Mbps:** about 1.5 hours.

In practice it can take longer, especially in the first hours, when millions of players download at once. That is why it pays to start as soon as preload opens.

## How to get ready before November 10

1. **Free up space now.** Open your console's storage settings and delete or move games you do not use. Aim for at least 230 GB free.
2. **Check where it will install.** PS5 games and Xbox Series X|S optimized games must go on internal storage or a compatible expansion drive, not a regular USB external drive.
3. **Update your console and confirm your purchase.** Keep the system up to date and make sure your digital pre-order shows in your library.
4. **Protect your connection.** If you can, use an Ethernet cable instead of Wi-Fi and set the console not to enter rest mode during the download.

## What about the physical edition?

Physical editions keep the **November 12** date and, as reported, include a **download code** instead of a disc. That means you will also download the full 200+ GB, so the advice about freeing space applies equally.

## What time does GTA 6 unlock on November 19?

Preload only lets you **download** the game: you cannot play until it unlocks on launch day. Rockstar has **not confirmed** the exact unlock time. Some outlets, citing PlayStation Store listings, point to midnight local time, but that is an unofficial estimate. We will update this article when there is an official announcement.

## Key takeaways

- Preload starts **November 10**, not the 12th (except the physical edition).
- The game will take **up to 212 GB**.
- The unlock time on November 19 is **not confirmed**.
