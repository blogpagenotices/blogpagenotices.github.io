## A free map and poster for fans

Rockstar announced that on **November 19**, launch day for **GTA 6**, it will add to its official website a **printable poster with the cover art and a map of Leonida**, as a free digital download. It is a detail many fans of the series were hoping for, because maps from earlier games came inside the box.

## How to get them for free

1. **Go to Rockstar Games' official website** on November 19.
2. **Look for the poster and Leonida map download.** Rockstar will publish them that day.
3. **Download and print them** at whatever size you like. For a large poster, the easiest option is to take the file to a print shop.

Until Rockstar publishes the exact link, be wary of pages promising the map early: the safe route is the official site.

## What about the physical poster?

Rockstar also announced a printed version:

- **Where:** on the **Rockstar Store**, shortly after launch.
- **Price:** **$0.99** on its own, or as a **free gift with any purchase in the store**.
- **Availability:** Rockstar has not detailed shipping to each country, so if you live outside the United States, check the store when it goes live.

## Does it come with the physical editions of the game?

No. Physical editions of GTA 6 go on sale from November 12, but they are a box with a **download code, no disc**, and the announcements we reviewed do not mention the map inside the box. To have the map on paper you need to print it or order the physical poster from the store.

## Why it matters

The Leonida map will be a useful reference from day one for finding your way around, and also for comparing the size and areas of the game with what has been seen in trailers. When Rockstar publishes it, we will break the map down on the blog.
