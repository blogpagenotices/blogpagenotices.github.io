## Three more clips, same day

The countdown to the Netflix event just got shaken up again. On August 25, CyberLeek posted three new videos almost simultaneously. The first shows **Jason riding a bike along the beach**, appearing to demonstrate movement physics and Leonida's coastal environment. The second places him inside a **fictional video game store**, a small open-world touch that's becoming a recurring feature in each leak. The third is the one that generated the most noise — and not exactly for technical reasons.

## The clip that crossed a different line

Today's third video shows Jason exploring what appears to be a nudist beach area at night, including nudity and a violent confrontation. Unlike previous clips, this one drew controversy for its explicit content rather than for revealing new game mechanics. We won't go into further detail on that particular clip — if you're interested in the full context, several gaming-focused outlets covered it the same day.

## Take-Two adds GitHub to its subpoena list

Take-Two's legal push keeps growing. On top of the subpoenas already filed against Microsoft, Discord, X, and Google, **GitHub** has now been added — another platform where digital traces helping identify the person or group behind the leaks might exist. Some users have already voiced concern about how broad Take-Two's data request is becoming, potentially affecting accounts with no real connection to the case.

## An (unconfirmed) teaser about Lucia

Amid the release of these clips, CyberLeek hinted at the possible existence of a video showing Lucia's prologue — which, if real, would be the first time leaked content focuses exclusively on her rather than Jason. For now it remains just a hint, with no published material backing it up.

## Two days to go

With **"Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look"** scheduled for August 27, the question we raised in our [previous article](/en/posts/8-cyberleek-token-cripto-dudas/) is more relevant than ever: how much genuinely "new" content does Rockstar have left to show at its own presentation, after a leak wave that now totals more than a dozen clips?
