## One wait inside another wait

If waiting until November for GTA 6 already hurt, here's more bad news for PC players: **the November 19, 2026 date is exclusive to PS5 and Xbox Series X|S**. Rockstar hasn't announced, not even remotely, when the PC version will arrive.

## Why do consoles go first?

It's not a coincidence, nor a lack of interest in PC. It's Rockstar's usual playbook: launch on consoles first to immediately reach the largest, most established player base, then take extra time to optimize the PC port afterward. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick hinted at this himself, noting that the PC market keeps growing and that practically all of the company's big hits eventually reach every platform, sooner or later.

## So, when does it come to PC?

Here's where we need to separate facts from speculation:

- **What's confirmed:** there's no date, not even an announcement that a PC version exists.
- **The historical pattern:** GTA V took about 18 months to reach PC after its console debut. Red Dead Redemption 2 followed a similar timeline. If GTA 6 repeats that pattern, we'd be looking at late 2027 or even 2028.
- **The rumor going around:** a leak from a social media user, attributed to former Rockstar employees, points to February 2027. That's a much more optimistic date than the historical pattern, and the leaker themselves clarified it's just an estimate that could change. Take it with a grain of salt.

## About pricing and editions (this part is official)

While we wait for PC news, this much is already confirmed for the console launch: the Standard Edition costs $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition $99.99. All pre-orders include the free "Vintage Vice City" pack. According to Take-Two, pre-orders for the Ultimate Edition are outpacing the standard edition, something the company reads as a sign of "global excitement" for the game.

## What can you do in the meantime?

If your plan is to play it on PC no matter what, the smart move is to hold off on buying hardware "for GTA 6" right now — Rockstar hasn't even published official requirements, and any spec chart you see floating around is an unofficial estimate. If you want to play from day one, the only confirmed path right now is PS5 or Xbox Series X|S.
