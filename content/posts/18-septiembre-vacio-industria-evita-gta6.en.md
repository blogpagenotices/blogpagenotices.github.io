## A void felt across the entire industry

There's still more than two months until November 19, but GTA 6's effect is already visible across the entire 2026 release calendar — and not in the way you'd expect. Instead of competing head-on with Rockstar, practically **no major studio** wants to launch its game anywhere near that date.

## September filled up to the brim

The result of this collective strategy of avoiding GTA 6 is a September as packed as few have ever been. Among the launches that decided to move earlier are Insomniac's **Marvel's Wolverine** (September 15), **Silent Hill: Townfall**, Remedy's **Control Resonant** (September 24), **Onimusha: Way of the Sword**, and **Blood of the Dawnwalker** (September 3). Even **Phantom Blade Zero**, which originally aimed for September, got pushed to October to avoid adding to the pileup.

## A calculated risk

Industry analysts point out that while this strategy makes sense, it carries its own risk: with so many big titles competing against each other in a single month, some of them might not hit the sales numbers they were hoping for, simply because players don't have the time (or the budget) to give attention to all of them at once. In simple terms, avoiding GTA 6 might be creating a different problem — just as real.

## The "GTA effect" isn't new, but this time it's stronger

Every mainline entry in the series has caused some shuffling in competitors' release schedules, but the scale of reaction this time — with an entire month practically cleared out around Rockstar's date — has no recent precedent. It's another sign that, as we already saw with the [pre-order surge](/en/posts/16-reservas-disparadas-extended-look/) following the Extended Look, GTA 6 has stopped being "just another launch" and become an event that reshapes how the entire industry moves around it.

## Will the strategy pay off?

We'll only know once the actual sales numbers come in for September and, later, November. For now, the only certainty is that no major studio wants to be the exception brave enough to go head-to-head with Rockstar this year.
