## Rockstar confirms GTA 6 performance

On November 10, 2026, the same day preload opened, Rockstar Games published on its Newswire how **GTA 6** will run on console. The key line: the game **targets 30 frames per second (30 FPS)** on all platforms. Here is what it said, what it means and what is still unknown.

## What Rockstar confirmed, by console

| Console | What we know |
|---|---|
| **PS5** | 30 FPS target. DualSense haptic feedback, Tempest 3D audio and improved load times. |
| **PS5 Pro** | 30 FPS target, with **improved clarity through PSSR** (Sony's AI upscaling) and **ray-traced shadows**, exclusive to this console. |
| **Xbox Series X\|S** | 30 FPS target across all platforms. Rockstar gave no modes or per-console settings. |
| **Xbox Cloud Gaming** | Rockstar highlights that the game can be played in the cloud, using Series S graphics settings. |

Rockstar also confirmed that **Remote Play** will be supported on PlayStation and Xbox.

## What "targeting 30 FPS" means

Rockstar uses the word "targeting", so this is a **performance goal**, not a guarantee that every second runs at exactly 30. Thirty FPS means 30 images per second, half of what 60 FPS modes deliver. In huge open-world games it is a common choice: Red Dead Redemption 2 also launched at 30 FPS on consoles.

The upside is more detail, more people and more objects on screen. The cost is that motion looks less fluid than at 60 FPS, which some players notice a lot when moving the camera.

## What Rockstar has not confirmed

- **Resolution:** the announcements we reviewed give no resolution figures for any console.
- **Graphics modes:** there is no mention of a performance or quality mode.
- **60 FPS mode:** neither confirmed nor ruled out. For now, the only official target is 30 FPS.
- **Xbox Series X vs Series S:** there is no breakdown of differences between them.

If Rockstar announces more before November 19, we will update this article.

1. **Check your TV settings.** Turn on game mode to reduce the delay between controller and screen.
2. **If you have a PS5 Pro, use its 4K output.** It is the console that gets the extra confirmed improvements.
