## Take-Two: pre-orders beat expectations

Ten days before launch, **Take-Two Interactive**, Rockstar's parent company, said on its November 9, 2026 earnings call that **GTA 6** pre-orders are running **far above what it expected**. It is the strongest signal so far of how big the launch could be, although the company itself urges caution. Here is what it said, which figures are official and which are not.

## What Take-Two said

- **Strauss Zelnick, CEO:** "Preorders for Grand Theft Auto VI are tracking far in excess of our initial expectations."
- **Lainie Goldstein, chief financial officer:** the level of pre-orders generated "is extraordinary".
- **Zelnick, on celebrating early:** "we still aren't ready to declare victory." According to Reuters, he added that the company tends to avoid claiming success before it happens.
- **Game status:** Zelnick said "Rockstar has completed the development of GTA VI". Launch patches remain possible.

Take-Two **did not publish a pre-order number**. According to 24/7 Wall St., the company's shares rose about 8% after the call.

## Which figures are official and which are not

| Item | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Fiscal 2027 net bookings: $8.0 billion to $8.2 billion | Take-Two (reiterated outlook) | Official |
| Fiscal Q3 2027 net bookings: $3.35 billion to $3.5 billion, versus $1.76 billion a year earlier | Take-Two (CFO) | Official (outlook) |
| About 11.27 million units sold as of November 12 | Sensor Tower | Estimate, unconfirmed |
| Nearly 90% of pre-orders through late August were the Ultimate edition | Sensor Tower, cited by Reuters | Estimate, unconfirmed |

Note the difference: the first two figures are **the company's own outlook**, and the last two are **estimates from an outside firm**. Neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has confirmed them.

## Why Take-Two is being so careful

According to Screen Rant, Zelnick explained that he does not know whether pre-orders predict real sales at launch, because some buyers are simply buying early and pre-orders can be cancelled. It makes sense: very high pre-orders are a good sign, but they do not guarantee how the game will perform once it is out.

## What about GTA 7? What the CEO did and did not say

In recent days the idea circulated that Zelnick said **GTA 7 will not take as long as GTA 6**, which arrived more than 13 years after GTA V. We checked the sources and **that is not supported**:

- **What he did say,** in an interview with IGN according to Notebookcheck and tbreak: "the nature and velocity of the content we'll see from our iconic franchises, including GTA, will look dramatically different over the next decade."
- **What he did not say:** he gave no date or timeframe for GTA 7, did not discuss development time and did not confirm a next game. The "content" he refers to could be new games, but also updates or GTA Online content.
- **On the earnings call,** Zelnick declined to give fiscal 2028 guidance and said what comes next will be announced "in the fullness of time".

In other words, some headlines went further than what he said. The only thing we can verify is that Take-Two expects changes in the pace of content across its franchises, with no concrete schedule.
