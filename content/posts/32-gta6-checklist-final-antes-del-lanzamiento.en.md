## Four days to GTA 6: check this before Thursday

GTA 6 launches on **November 19, 2026**. If you made it this far, the only job left is not to trip on the last stretch. This list puts the essentials in one place. Each item links to the article that explains it in more detail, with its sources.

## The checklist

1. **Free space.** The game takes up to **212 GB** by the official figure, and the update adds to that. Leave some extra margin, since some stores have shown slightly different sizes. If you are short on space, see [how to free up space on PS5 and Xbox](/posts/26-gta6-liberar-espacio-ps5-xbox/).
2. **Preload finished.** Preload has been open since November 10. Check on your console that the game shows as ready to play and not as a paused download. Details in [when preload starts, by platform](/posts/24-gta6-precarga-10-noviembre-212-gb/).
3. **Update 01.002.000 installed.** It is about 1 GB and, according to several outlets, required to play. It rolls out in phases, so if it has not shown up, check manually. Everything in [the first update](/posts/31-gta6-primera-actualizacion-1-002-000/).
4. **Unlock time.** According to the Rockstar support page that Forbes cited, the game unlocks **at midnight local time on November 19** in every region. Find your country's exact time in [what time GTA 6 comes out](/posts/25-gta6-a-que-hora-sale-por-pais/).
5. **If you bought the physical edition.** Physical editions went on sale on November 12 and, as reported, come with a **download code rather than a disc**. Redeem it early and do not wait for launch day. There is also no physical Ultimate edition.
6. **Account and connection.** Check that your PlayStation Network or Xbox sign-in works, that your console has a stable connection and that automatic updates are on.
7. **Digital purchase extras.** Rockstar announced the **Vintage Vice City Pack** and **one month of GTA+** for anyone who buys the digital version before November 20. Make sure your purchase qualifies.
8. **Which edition you chose.** Standard costs **$79.99** and Ultimate **$99.99**. If you are still undecided, review [Standard vs. Ultimate](/posts/28-gta6-estandar-vs-ultimate/).
9. **Expect 30 FPS.** The performance target is **30 frames per second** on all consoles, with enhancements on PS5 Pro. It is covered in [GTA 6 at 30 FPS on PS5 Pro](/posts/27-gta6-30-fps-ps5-pro/).
10. **Map and poster.** Rockstar promised a free poster and map download on November 19, and a physical poster for $0.99 at its store. More in [the Leonida map and poster](/posts/29-gta6-mapa-poster-leonida-gratis/).

## The night before

- Turn on your console and let any pending update finish.
- Restart your router if your connection tends to drop.
- Charge your controllers and clear your schedule: the unlock is at midnight and demand will be high.
- Be patient with the servers in the first hours. Queues or errors on day one are normal for a launch this size.

## What we still do not know

Rockstar has not confirmed whether more updates will arrive before launch or what 01.002.000 contains. We will update this article when there is news.
