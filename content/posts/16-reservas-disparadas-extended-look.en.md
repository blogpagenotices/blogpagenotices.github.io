## From hype to actual purchases

We already knew GTA 6 was generating enormous hype. What the latest data confirms is that this hype is translating directly into sales, and at a speed that surprised even analysts who've been tracking the phenomenon for months.

## The exact numbers

According to **Sensor Tower**, a market analytics firm, GTA 6 pre-orders jumped **436% one day after** the Extended Look aired on Netflix, compared to the average of the previous 30 days. That momentum didn't fade the next day: over a full two days, the increase reached **572%**. These numbers far exceed any pre-order spike the game had seen before, including the already impressive figures from previous months.

## It wasn't just the game that spiked

The impact was felt on multiple fronts at once. The Netflix app climbed **61 spots** on Apple's App Store ranking in just 24 hours, going from an average rank of 76 to position 35. Overall platform traffic rose 125% compared to the previous day, with a 50% jump specifically in mobile usage. And on YouTube, the official Extended Look upload has already racked up around **17 million views**.

## The side effect nobody saw coming: PS5 Pro sells out

One of the more curious details in all this: the surge in interest was so large that **PlayStation 5 Pro** stock started running out at several retailers, with some third-party sellers jacking up resale prices to as much as $1,300. It's a pretty direct sign that a significant chunk of players aren't just pre-ordering the game — they're upgrading their hardware specifically to have it ready by November.

## A move that raised eyebrows, and now results

It's worth remembering that giving Netflix the temporary exclusive on the video drew plenty of criticism when it was announced — as we covered in our [analysis of the Netflix partnership](/en/posts/12-netflix-alianza-historica/). Four days later, the numbers suggest the bet paid off exactly as Rockstar and Take-Two expected: more reach, more conversation, and now, more confirmed sales.

## What's next

With Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick comparing the Extended Look to just "one of the appetizers" of the full marketing campaign, everything points to these figures being only the beginning of what's coming before November 19.
