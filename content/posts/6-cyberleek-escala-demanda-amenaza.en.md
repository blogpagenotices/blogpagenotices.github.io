## From protest to court case

What started as a leak with an attached manifesto just took a serious turn: according to multiple outlets, **Take-Two Interactive filed several subpoenas with the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of New York on August 20**, demanding that Microsoft and Discord hand over information that could help identify the person or group behind CyberLeek. This is no longer just about copyright takedowns — it's a formal investigation aimed at a real identity.

## Nine leaks and counting

Since the wave began on August 18, CyberLeek has published around nine separate videos, each showing gameplay fragments: chases, a basketball minigame, melee fights, and even a clip inside a nightclub. In one of the most recent videos, Jason is seen shooting a wall to spell out the word "Leek" with bullet holes — a fairly direct signature from the group.

## The most serious threat yet

The most concerning twist came after the legal pressure hit: CyberLeek warned that if "something happens to them" (a clear reference to being identified or arrested), **the full game would be leaked, including a hypothetical PC version** that doesn't even have an official release date. It's a classic "dead man's switch" threat — used to discourage authorities from acting by presenting an automatic retaliation as a guarantee.

## The site starts going dark

According to recent reports, CyberLeek's website and Telegram channel — which had become the central hub for distributing clips and the "manifesto" — **started being blocked in various countries**, and the group has gone quiet in recent days. Some analysts read this as a sign the legal pressure is working; others warn it could just be a pause before a bigger leak, exactly as the group promised.

## The detail nobody should overlook: the cryptocurrency

As we covered in our [previous CyberLeek article](/en/posts/4-cyberleek-filtracion-gta6/), the group created its own crypto token to fund what it calls a "secret project" whose details haven't been revealed. According to blockchain analysis, the funds raised are around $27,000. It's public, traceable information — anyone can verify the transactions — but where that money is actually going remains a total mystery.

## Does this affect the launch?

For now, not at all. The **November 19, 2026** date remains firm, and Rockstar is still preparing its presentation, **"Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look,"** scheduled for August 27 on Netflix at 3:00 PM Eastern Time. What is in question is how much genuinely "fresh" information will be left for that presentation if leaks keep coming at this pace.
