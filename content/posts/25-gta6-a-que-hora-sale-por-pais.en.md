## The short answer

**GTA 6 launches on November 19, 2026**, but it does not unlock everywhere at the same time. According to PlayStation Store listings, the game unlocks at **12:00 a.m. (midnight) on November 19, local time in each country**. In practice, for most of the Americas and Europe that means **the night of Wednesday the 18th into Thursday the 19th**.

One important caveat: **Rockstar has not published an unlock time of its own**. The information comes from the stores, so treat it as very likely, but not as an official announcement. Below we explain what is confirmed and what is not.

## What time does GTA 6 come out in your country

This table shows the local unlock time, based on store data. If you have preloaded the game, you only need to wait for that time to play.

| Country | Unlock time (local) |
|---|---|
| **United States (East Coast)** | 12:00 a.m., Thursday, November 19 |
| **United States (West Coast)** | 9:00 p.m., Wednesday, November 18 |
| **Canada** | 12:00 a.m., Thursday, November 19 (Eastern time) |
| **UK, Ireland, Portugal** | 12:00 a.m., Thursday, November 19 |
| **France, Germany, Italy, Spain** | 12:00 a.m., Thursday, November 19 |
| **Mexico and Central America** | 12:00 a.m., Thursday, November 19 (Mexico City time) |
| **Colombia, Peru, Ecuador, Panama** | 12:00 a.m., Thursday, November 19 |
| **Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Uruguay, Paraguay** | 12:00 a.m., Thursday, November 19 |
| **India** | 12:00 a.m., Thursday, November 19 |
| **Japan, South Korea** | 12:00 a.m., Thursday, November 19 |
| **Australia, New Zealand** | 12:00 a.m., Thursday, November 19 |

Each country gets a single unlock time in the store. That is why countries with several time zones differ: in the United States, for example, the West Coast gets it at 9:00 p.m. on the 18th.

## Who gets to play first?

Because unlock happens at local midnight, the game arrives in waves. According to store data, **New Zealand, Australia and Japan** play first, on November 18 in UTC terms. Continental Europe follows, then the UK, and the **Americas come among the last**: Argentina and Brazil first, then Colombia, Peru, the US and Canada, and finally Mexico and Central America.

## What is confirmed and what is not

- **Confirmed:** the November 19, 2026 date, for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- **Seen in the store, not announced by Rockstar:** the local-midnight unlock on PlayStation Store.
- **Unconfirmed:** Xbox. Its web page shows only the date, with no time, although the store's internal data points the same way in many countries.
- **Unconfirmed:** that a given country will not have a different time than expected. If your country is not in the table, check the game's page in your store.

We will update this article when Rockstar makes an official announcement.

## How to be ready at that time

The key is to reach midnight with everything downloaded:

1. **Use preload.** It starts November 10 and the game takes up to 212 GB. Full details in our guide on [when preload starts, by platform](/en/posts/24-gta6-precarga-10-noviembre-212-gb/).
2. **Free up space early.** Without 230 GB free, you cannot start the download.
3. **Check that your pre-order is in your library.** Do it days ahead, not at the last minute.
4. **Expect a traffic spike.** Many people will log in at midnight. A launch patch or a server queue could delay you by a few minutes.
