## Finally, real gameplay

After years of waiting, cinematic trailers, and a leak wave that threatened to ruin the surprise, Rockstar finally showed GTA 6 in motion. The "Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look" event streamed today on Netflix with **26 minutes of footage**, captured entirely on PlayStation 5.

## The scene: looking for Boobie

The video opens with Jason and Lucia entering an apartment building, looking for someone named "Boobie" to make a pickup. Things escalate quickly: the two split up, and what starts as a routine job turns into a shootout inside the building.

## The combat that surprised everyone

Here's the detail the community is talking about the most: the combat system looks notably snappier and more responsive than in previous GTA entries, with a feel several journalists directly compared to **Max Payne 3**. At one point in the video, Jason triggers a kind of tactical slow-motion to land several headshots in a row — a mechanic never before confirmed in the mainline series.

## Lucia doesn't need guns to be lethal

While Jason takes the elevator down, Lucia splits off and ends up in a hand-to-hand fight with an attacker in a stairwell after losing her weapons. The sequence shows her taking a hard hit to the head, being strangled for a few seconds, and finally overpowering her attacker to grab his gun. It's the clearest confirmation yet of what we already covered in our [protagonist profile](/en/posts/5-jason-lucia-personajes-gta6/): Lucia is a trained fighter, not a decorative sidekick in the story.

## A technical detail that sparked debate

Rockstar confirmed the demo ran on PS5, and conversation has already started about the frame rate shown — some outlets note it could be improved with a patch before or after launch, something fairly common for a game of this scale.

## What's next

With this, much of the uncertainty generated by the recent leaks was resolved by Rockstar itself, on its own terms. We'll keep breaking down the rest of the material in upcoming articles — stay tuned.
