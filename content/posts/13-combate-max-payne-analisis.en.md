## A problem GTA had been dragging for years

Anyone who's played GTA V honestly knows its combat, while functional, was never the series' strong suit: clunky aiming, unreliable cover, and shootouts that felt more like a formality than a polished mechanic. What was shown today in the Extended Look suggests Rockstar finally decided to fix this at the root.

## The comparison everyone's making

Several specialized outlets landed on the same observation the moment the stream ended: GTA 6's combat feels **directly inspired by Max Payne 3**, Rockstar's 2012 shooter known for its precise gunplay and iconic use of bullet time. In the scene shown, Jason triggers a similar mechanic to land several headshots in a row — something that had never been confirmed before in a mainline GTA.

## Why this is a meaningful shift

Max Payne 3 isn't just another title in Rockstar's catalog — for many players and critics, it's the most polished third-person shooter the company has ever made. Bringing that combat DNA into GTA 6 suggests Rockstar is treating gunplay with the same seriousness it's applied to narrative and open-world design in its recent releases, rather than leaving it as an afterthought system.

## Melee combat gets an upgrade too

It's not just about firearms. The sequence of Lucia fighting in a stairwell — taking hits, getting strangled, and finally overpowering her attacker — shows hand-to-hand combat choreography with more weight and physical consequence than any fistfight previously seen in the series.

## Enough to compete with other open-world shooters?

That's an open question until the game is actually in players' hands. But the comparison to Max Payne 3 alone — a very high bar within Rockstar's own catalog — already sets different expectations than existed before the Extended Look. If the rest of the game holds this level of polish across its systems, GTA 6 could stop being criticized for its combat for the first time in the series' history.
