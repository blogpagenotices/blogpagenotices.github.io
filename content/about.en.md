**GTA 6 News** is an independent fan-run site dedicated to covering news, guides, theories, and analysis about Grand Theft Auto VI as its release approaches.

## What you'll find here

- Verified news about release date, pricing, editions, and official announcements
- Guides and summaries of everything confirmed so far
- Community analysis and theories, always clearly labeled as speculation when they're not official information
